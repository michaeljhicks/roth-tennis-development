A player-first approach

Technique matters.
So does the person.

Every lesson starts with the participant—not a cookie-cutter curriculum. Goals, ability, confidence, and the reason you came to the court all matter.

Start with you

Check in, establish the goal for the session, and understand what progress should look and feel like for you.

Build the foundation

Develop repeatable fundamentals, movement, decision-making, and an understanding of how the pieces of your game connect.

Leave with confidence

Turn better mechanics into self-belief, competitive readiness, and the courage to keep growing between lessons.

Good to know

Before you
step on court.

What should I expect when I arrive for a lesson?

The session begins with a conversation to check in and establish the goal for the lesson. From there, you’ll warm up before moving into the main instruction and drills.

Where are lessons located?

Lessons are offered at multiple locations across the greater Grand Rapids area. After you book, Roth Tennis Development will contact you to confirm the location that is most convenient for your lesson.

What equipment should I bring?

Wear comfortable athletic clothing and non-marking tennis shoes, and bring water plus sunscreen for outdoor sessions. Bring a racket if you have one; a racket can be provided if you do not.

Do I need tennis experience to participate?

No. Roth Tennis Development serves participants across different capabilities, and Tennis Fit specifically requires no tennis experience. The right program depends on your goals and what you want to get out of the session.

How does booking work?

Choose a lesson, select an available date and time, submit your contact information, and complete the appropriate registration form for your first session. Roth Tennis Development then confirms your lesson location.
